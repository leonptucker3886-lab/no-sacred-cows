The Long Way Home

Preconception

We were created as humans — fully formed, discontinuous from the rest of nature, at the center of the story.

If the story of us begins with a finished human, dropped into a finished world, then deep time is scenery. The evidence says deep time is the story — and that we are a late chapter, not the title page.

From sea to us — a walk of form

Life did not leap from rock to upright primate. It accumulated. Tap the path.

1. Early vertebrates in water

Embryos still carry the receipt

Development is not a perfect replay of evolution — but human embryos still show structures that make sense only if we inherited a long vertebrate past.

Not Haeckel’s slogan. “Ontogeny recapitulates phylogeny” as a strict law is outdated. Modern developmental biology is subtler: embryos of related species share early pathways and modular toolkits because they share ancestors — including stages that look fish-like before they look human.

If we were “created,” at what stage?

  • Physics first — particles, stars, heavier elements (you are partly star-stuff; that is literal).
  • Then chemistry and life — single cells for most of Earth’s history.
  • Then animals, then water vertebrates, then land — the walk above.
  • Then primates, then Homo — tools, fire, larger brains.
  • Then us — a thin slice of the total record, still developing the same deep body plan.

We are still changing

Monkeys are distant cousins — not grandparents

  • Humans and chimpanzees / bonobos share a common ancestor a few million years ago — cousins, not ancestors.
  • Other monkeys branch off earlier — shared great-great-grandparents on the tree, not a ladder from today’s monkey to you.
  • All of us sit inside primates, mammals, vertebrates. The tree is nested.

None of this is here to cancel religion

Religion is one of the main ways humans have organized awe, grief, justice, and belonging. It built hospitals and universities, held communities through famine and war, and gave ordinary people a language for the feeling that the world is larger than a paycheck.

That beauty does not require the universe to be small or young. Facts about galaxies, fins-to-limbs, and embryonic arches are not a demand that anyone stop praying. They are a demand that the map of nature not be rewritten to flatter us.

Voices who kept the wonder

"The cosmos is within us. We are made of star-stuff. We are a way for the universe to know itself."— Carl Sagan
"We are like butterflies who flutter for a day and think it is forever."— Carl Sagan
"The universe is under no obligation to make sense to you."— Neil deGrasse Tyson
"We are part of this universe; we are in this universe, but perhaps more important than both of those facts, is that the universe is in us."— Neil deGrasse Tyson

Late arrivals on a small world, related to the rest of life, still changing, still capable of awe. That is the long way home — and it is enough to take seriously.
