Commonwealth vs State

Preconception

A commonwealth functions differently than a state when it comes to courts, punishment, and laws.

Four U.S. jurisdictions call themselves commonwealths: Virginia, Massachusetts, Pennsylvania, and Kentucky. For courts, crime, punishment, and lawmaking, a commonwealth is a state with a leftover name.

Where the word comes from

In early modern English, commonwealth meant the public good. When these states adopted constitutions, some chose "Commonwealth" in the formal title. That was rhetorical and historical — not a parallel legal system.

Myth vs fact

Puerto Rico is the confusion magnet. Its status debates are real. Virginia is not Puerto Rico. Collapsing them turns a brand name into a theory of sovereignty those states do not claim.

Bottom line

Commonwealth is a historical label. It does not create different courts, different punishment physics, or an escape from law. Branding is not architecture. The architecture is the ordinary American state.
